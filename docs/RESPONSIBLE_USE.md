# Responsible use

* **Level of detail.** The catalogue, the atlas and the prompts contain mechanism-level descriptions only: no signal specifications, parameters, procedures, code or tooling. The atlas holds high-level summaries of published work with links to the sources.
  The aim is defensive analysis without lowering the effort needed to attack a system.
* **What the data says and does not say.** The analysis is architectural and concerns a published reference design. A score ranks a threat inside this study; it does not measure the likelihood of an attack on any real system.
* **Review prompts.** The prompts ask the models for classification, rating and reasoning about risk, and instruct them not to give attack procedures. Model replies are included unedited; they are the models' own words.
* **Pre-publication data in third-party services.** The prompts were sent to commercial services. They contain mechanism-level content only; check each provider's data-use terms before sending further material.
* **Do not leak the answer key.** If you repeat the blind review, do not put the catalogue workbook or anything from it into a chat before the blind runs are complete.
* **Reporting.** If you find an error, an inappropriate level of detail, or a wrong attribution, please open an issue or write to the contact in the README.
